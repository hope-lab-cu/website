Resources.

Explore literature-referenced laboratory method guides and analytical workflows used in HOPE Lab research and student training at the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. This collection covers microbial culture and preservation concepts, antimicrobial susceptibility testing standards, LAB aggregation assays, E24 emulsification, 16S rRNA amplicon analysis with QIIME 2 and DADA2, DPPH antioxidant assessment, and hydrolytic enzyme screening. Each document identifies its scientific basis, key controls, interpretation considerations and source references; bench procedures remain subject to current supervisor-approved institutional SOPs.

METHODS USED IN HOPE LAB

Protocols, Methods and Research Workflows

A collection of laboratory and analytical methods employed in HOPE Lab research and used in student training. Protocols may have been adapted to specific organisms, samples, instruments and experimental objectives. Their suitability and reproducibility under different conditions have not necessarily been established. Researchers using these materials outside HOPE Lab should independently assess and validate the methods and assume responsibility for their application and interpretation.

Each PDF cites the foundational paper, peer-reviewed method source or current technical standard used to prepare the guide. The 16S rRNA/QIIME 2/DADA2 document provides a step-by-step computational workflow with commands; wet-lab documents are educational method references, not executable bench SOPs, and do not replace supervisor-approved laboratory procedures.

Microbial Culture Media, Colony Isolation, Purification and Preservation

Objective: Explain the roles of culture-media selection, colony isolation, purity assessment and preservation documentation in supervised microbiology research.
