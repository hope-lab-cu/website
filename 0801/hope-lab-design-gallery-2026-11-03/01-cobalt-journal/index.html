Discovery to Impact

We explore how organisms, functional biomolecules, and biological pathways influence health, ecological function, and environmental sustainability.

HOPE Lab (Laboratory for Health, Omics and Pathway Exploration) is based in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong, Chattogram, Bangladesh. We explore how organisms, functional biomolecules, and biological pathways influence how biological systems function, adapt, and interact with their environments.

02 / RESEARCH AT A GLANCE

Questions across
biological systems.

From fermented foods and functional molecules to molecular mechanisms and ecological context, our work connects biochemical discovery with broader biological questions.

01 / THEME

Probiotics, Fermented Foods & Functional Foods

We study fermented foods and probiotic microorganisms, and their health-promoting molecules, to inform the development of functional foods, with emphasis on food quality, safety, nutritional value, and public health.

Explore theme ↗
02 / THEME

Microbial Functional Molecules

We investigate exopolysaccharides, pigments, biosurfactants, enzymes, antimicrobial compounds, and other functional metabolites, examining their biochemical properties, biological activities, and potential applications.

Explore theme ↗
03 / THEME

Multi-Omics & Bioinformatics

We integrate genomics, glycomics, and metabolomics with bioinformatics to investigate molecular characteristics, functional potential, and biochemical processes in microorganisms and other biological systems.
