TJH

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dr. Tanim Jabid Hossain

Associate Professor · Principal Investigator

Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh

Dr. Hossain directs HOPE Lab’s research on biochemical processes, bioactive molecules and molecular mechanisms in microorganisms, examining their functions and interactions across interconnected biological systems. His research integrates microbial biochemistry with genomics, glycomics, metabolomics and related approaches.

JOIN THE LAB

Opportunities for prospective students

Students interested in research in microbial biochemistry, biotechnology, probiotics, functional biomolecules, genomics, and related fields are welcome to inquire about opportunities. We encourage students to have a genuine reason for pursuing research and a clear idea of how the experience may contribute to achieving their life goals. Opportunities depend on ongoing projects, available supervision, and laboratory capacity. Integrity, responsibility, and a willingness to learn are essential.
