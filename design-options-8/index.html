How to review these concepts

Open each homepage and then inspect its Research, Publications & Preprints, People and Contact pages. Compare how it handles long scientific paragraphs, publication records, navigation, content hierarchy and mobile screens. The concepts use different typographic directions; Inter is used as a light-weight preference in the Swiss and Pelagic systems where available, with system fallbacks.

These are complete design previews, not yet the CMS-editable version. After selection, the chosen system can be adapted for Pages CMS.
