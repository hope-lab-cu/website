HOPE LAB — SIX ADDITIONAL WEBSITE DESIGN CONCEPTS

This package contains a separate preview folder named design-options-6.
It does not replace the existing website or the earlier design-previews folder.

Inside are six complete website concepts. Each concept contains:
- index.html (Home)
- research.html
- publications.html
- people.html
- contact.html
- assets/style.css
- assets/site.js
- assets/hope-logo.webp

The gallery is design-options-6/index.html.
The content is based on the HOPE Lab website draft supplied by the user and the previous HTML preview. The layouts are responsive and use local files only; no external font or image service is required for the design to render.

Concepts:
04 Forest Observatory — dark forest green and mint
05 Carbon & Copper — charcoal and copper
06 Deep Ocean Matrix — dark scientific blue and cyan
07 Ivory & Forest Editorial — warm ivory and evergreen
08 Swiss Research Grid — white, cool grey and cobalt
09 Sage & Graphite — pale sage, graphite and clay

These are design previews, not yet configured for Pages CMS. Once a design is selected, its content can be separated into editable files and connected to Pages CMS.
