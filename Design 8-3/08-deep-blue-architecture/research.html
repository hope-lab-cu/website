HOPE LAB / RESEARCH
DISCOVERY TO IMPACT

Research across organisms, molecules, and systems.

HOPE Lab explores how organisms, functional biomolecules, and biological pathways influence how biological systems function, adapt, and interact with their environments. Our 1H → 2O → 3P → 4E framework connects One Health challenges with molecular investigation and ecological context.

RESEARCH THEMES

From molecular function
to wider context.

Our research combines biochemical and microbiological investigation with omics and computational approaches.

01 / RESEARCH AREA

Functional biomolecules

Biological systems produce a diverse range of molecules with distinct biochemical properties and functions. We investigate microbial exopolysaccharides, pigments, biosurfactants, enzymes, antimicrobial compounds, and other functional metabolites, examining their characteristics, biological activities, and underlying functions. Through biochemical and microbiological approaches, we explore their potential relevance to food preservation, biotechnology, agriculture, and environmental management.

02 / RESEARCH AREA

One Health

Health is shaped by interconnected biological processes and interactions across hosts, food systems, and environments. Our research examines beneficial microorganisms, particularly lactic acid bacteria associated with fermented foods, alongside microbial characteristics relevant to host interactions, food safety, and antimicrobial resistance. We investigate probiotic properties, antimicrobial activities, functional metabolites, and other biological traits to better understand the contributions of these organisms to health.

03 / RESEARCH AREA

Multi-omics

Understanding biological function often requires examining multiple levels of molecular organisation. We integrate genomics, glycomics, and metabolomics, supported by bioinformatics, to investigate functional potential, metabolic characteristics, and biochemical processes in microorganisms and other biological systems. These complementary approaches help reveal molecular features associated with adaptation, biological capabilities, and activity.

04 / RESEARCH AREA

Biological and ecological context

At HOPE Lab, we seek to understand not only what biological systems can do, but also the biochemical mechanisms, molecular features, and ecological interactions underlying their functions. We combine experimental biochemistry and microbiology with genomic, molecular, and computational approaches to investigate biological questions from different perspectives.
