Principal Investigator

TJHPI, HOPE Lab, CU

Dr. Tanim Jabid Hossain

Associate Professor · Founder & Principal Investigator

Dr. Tanim Jabid Hossain is an Associate Professor in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, and Founder and Principal Investigator of HOPE Lab. A microbial biochemist, his research focuses on microbial metabolism, glycobiology and functional biomolecules, including N-linked glycans, microbial glycan-processing pathways and bioactive microbial products. He undertook doctoral research through the RIKEN–Saitama University Joint Program in Japan and served as an International Program Associate with RIKEN's Glycometabolome Team from 2012 to 2015. As Founder and Principal Investigator of HOPE Lab, he directs research combining experimental biochemistry and microbiology with genomics, glycomics and metabolomics to investigate microbial biochemical pathways, molecular functions and biological interactions across host, food and environmental systems.

PEOPLE AND OPPORTUNITIES

Student Research Opportunities

Students interested in research in microbial biochemistry, biotechnology, probiotics, functional biomolecules, genomics, and related fields are welcome to inquire about opportunities. We encourage students to have a genuine reason for pursuing research and a clear idea of how the experience may contribute to achieve their life goal. Opportunities depend on ongoing projects, available supervision, and laboratory capacity. Integrity, responsibility, and a willingness to learn are essential.
