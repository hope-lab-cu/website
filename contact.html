SELECTED RESEARCH CAPABILITIES

Research support

HOPE Lab welcomes enquiries from researchers seeking potential collaboration or technical input in selected areas of microbial genomics, bioinformatics, microbiology and biochemical analysis. The capabilities below outline areas in which the laboratory may contribute to a defined research project, subject to scientific fit, methodological suitability, available capacity, required approvals and an agreed scope of work.

Microbial genome analysis and functional genomics

Computational characterization of microbial genome datasets can help clarify genetic content, predicted functions and relationships among strains, providing a basis for biological interpretation and follow-up research.

  • Microbial genome sequence annotation
  • Gene and functional annotation
  • Comparative genomic and pathway-level analysis

Antimicrobial assessment and MIC analysis

Laboratory-based evaluation of microbial inhibitory effects can support the investigation of candidate strains and preparations in relation to selected test organisms and specific research questions.

  • Antimicrobial activity screening
  • Minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) assessment
  • Appropriate assay controls and documented procedures

Characterization of bioactive properties

These investigations focus on the biological effects and functional properties of candidate materials, interpreted in relation to their intended research context and the evidence generated.

  • Screening of biological extracts
  • Assessment of microbial products and culture supernatants
  • Bioactivity testing with appropriate controls

Hydrolytic enzyme screening and activity assays

Enzyme profiling can help characterize microbial isolates and biological preparations by examining their capacity to act on selected substrates, through qualitative screening and, where appropriate, quantitative activity measurements.

  • Agar-plate screening for amylase, protease and cellulase
  • Screening of other hydrolytic enzymes using suitable substrate-based assays
  • Quantitative enzyme activity assays, where appropriate to the study design and available resources
Scope and feasibility These areas outline potential research contributions. The relevance and feasibility of a proposed activity are considered individually in relation to the sample or data type, study design, technical requirements, available resources and institutional approvals.

SCIENTIFIC APPROACH

Methodological transparency and research integrity

HOPE Lab places importance on methodological transparency, traceable records, appropriate controls and evidence-based interpretation. These principles inform our approach to experimental work, data analysis and scientific reporting.

Methodological clarity

Research questions are approached with attention to suitable methods, appropriate controls and the requirements of the specific study.

Traceable research practice

Attention to sample and data provenance, experimental observations and analytical procedures supports reproducibility and clear interpretation.

Evidence-based interpretation

Findings are considered in the context of the available evidence, with relevant limitations and uncertainties reflected in scientific reporting.

Responsible scientific reporting: We aim to interpret and communicate findings in the context of the methods used and the evidence available, while taking care to acknowledge relevant limitations.

RESEARCH-RELATED CORRESPONDENCE

Research-related enquiries

For enquiries concerning the research areas outlined above, a brief description of the scientific question and the relevant organism, sample, sequence data or analytical need can help establish whether the topic aligns with the laboratory's current expertise and capacity.
